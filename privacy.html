Own Storage – Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026.

What the app can access

When you sign in with Google, Own Storage asks only for access to the files it creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What is stored where

Your saves are copied to your own Google Drive. The sign-in token and the list of synced saves stay on your console.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Own Storage has no server of its own: it talks to Google directly. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app downloads its sign-in configuration from this site, which GitHub hosts; GitHub's privacy statement applies to that download.

Google user data

Own Storage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder in your Drive removes your backups.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/lmipsum/own-storage/issues.
